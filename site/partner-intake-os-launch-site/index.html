13 / FAQ

Operating
Questions.

What is Partner Intake OS?+

An AI-powered partner operations assistant that captures partner signals from forms, email, ChatGPT, and connected systems and turns them into CRM records, tracking links, tasks, resources, activity events, and next actions.

Who is it designed for?+

Operators managing brokers, referral partners, affiliates, professional centers of influence, community partners, creators, fintech relationships, and strategic partnerships.

Does it approve partners automatically?+

No. It can recommend classifications and flag issues, but important partnership decisions can remain subject to human review.

What can I give it?+

Partner applications, intake submissions, notes, referral introductions, broker information, CRM data, or other relevant context.

What does it return?+

Depending on the request: partner records, CRM updates, tracking links, activity events, resources, campaign recommendations, follow-up tasks, onboarding actions, and structured next steps.

Is it only for funding brokers?+

No. The architecture supports brokers, referral partners, affiliates, CPAs, attorneys, business brokers, creators, community organizations, fintech companies, and strategic partners.

Is this the same thing as submitting a funding application?+

No. Partner Intake OS evaluates partners and partner relationships. It should not be confused with borrower or funding-applicant underwriting.

Can it connect to other systems?+

The broader architecture is designed around API-based workflows that can support systems such as Tally, Vercel, HubSpot, Notion, Google Sheets, and a future Partner Command Center. Specific connections depend on implementation.
